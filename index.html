Common questions
🔔

My reminders don’t arrive

Allow notifications for Kipumi in iOS Settings → Notifications → Kipumi, and check that a Focus is not holding them back.

📍

A reminder at a place doesn’t come

Kipumi needs location “While Using the App”: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Kipumi.

🌙

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone; there is no account. Settings → Export my data, or Settings → Erase everything.

✨

Restoring Kipumi Plus

On the Plus screen, tap “Restore purchases” with the Apple ID you bought it with.

🗓️

Cancelling Kipumi Plus

Apple manages subscriptions: iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Kipumi.

Privacy

Everything you give Kipumi stays on your iPhone. No account, no ads, no tracking. Read the Privacy Policy →
